# NX Port Studio 1.6.0 — Release Candidate 7

I prepared **RC7** (`v1.6.0-rc.7`, Studio **1.6.0 build 24**) to correct reproducible problems found while qualifying freshly rebuilt game ports. I keep the candidate experimental; a successful export, service connection or lobby entry does not establish a completed online match.

## Changes

- I prepare graphics-tracked guest memory immediately before each native socket receive, including attempts after a readiness wait or a partial stream read. A kernel copyout cannot invoke the runtime's user-space dirty-page fault handler: a valid tracked destination could previously fail with `EFAULT`. I reproduced that failure with the real dirty-page tracker and actual loopback TCP/UDP. I preserve errors for invalid and unregistered read-only destinations. This change does not add blind receive retries or make preparation atomic against concurrent re-protection.
- I recorded Smash 13.0.5's observed `near=0` depth mode in its profile. My complete RC6 export had bundled `near=-w` pipeline bases, which the runtime correctly rejected; it then rebuilt 31,148 bases in 217 seconds on this Mac. New offline archives use the observed mode. Runtime depth handling, archive validation and shader chain `a7dbbce711c3` remain unchanged. I still need to qualify a freshly packaged archive with the corrected stamp.
- I removed online account authorization from local presence initialization. The initializer now validates the SDK layout and an existing local UID, then copies that UID without credential reads or HTTP requests. My synthetic social fixture passed 174 checks in normal and ASan/UBSan runs, including offline modes, absent accounts, invalid UIDs and untouched object fields. Publishing presence remains authenticated and can still fail; this change does not make every subsequent account or social operation noninteractive.

I include the existing background execution, fullscreen presentation policy, LAN/LDN, signed iOS export and Nextendo integration from [RC6](1.6.0-rc.6.md). Updating Studio does not replace game apps already installed: I must rebuild and reinstall each port to include runtime changes.

## Current game qualification

I rebuilt **Mario Kart 8 Deluxe 4.0.1 for Mac** from the exact RC6 source, verified its signature and preserved its saves. Account availability persisted across restarts and the NEX service completed verified TLS. In the preceding candidate I entered a worldwide lobby with seven participants, then received a connection error while the course was loading. I have not completed a Nextendo race or observed the tracked-memory `EFAULT` in that game, so I do not attribute its disconnect to the receive fix.

I also replaced my installed **Smash 13.0.5 for Mac** with a complete modern export from RC6, including rewritten modules and matching modern shaders. It passed Play and its real pipeline preparation with zero failed bases. A later startup wait reached Keychain access through presence initialization; no authorization dialog was visible in my inspection. I have not yet measured fullscreen performance or repeated an online round with that export. My earlier real arena round remains historical evidence, as described in the [game note](../../dm/games/01006A800016E000/README.md#studio-runtime-qualification-2026-11-02).

My Splatoon 2 lobby/queue and background results, unresolved character colors and original Mii editor work retain the scope recorded in RC6. I do not transfer those results to other games, newer ports or iOS. My completed Mario Kart 3.0.5 Mac–Switch LAN race and LAN/LDN tests across native Mac/iOS ports and physical Switch hardware also remain historical qualification of those tested builds.

## Validation and distribution

I passed the complete host suite in normal and ASan/UBSan builds after the receive change, including all affected fixture links. The dedicated real-tracker receive fixture passed 43 checks in each build; invalid-memory checks retained `EFAULT`. I also passed documentation/profile checks. Remote CI, the final Studio package and physical game acceptance are separate release gates.

I publish source, original project artwork and approved Studio captures. Games, extracted resources, firmware, console keys, SDK binaries, credentials, captured service certificates and personal saves remain outside the repository and release. Game IPAs continue to require the creator's Xcode signing; the Studio Mac package has its separate signature.

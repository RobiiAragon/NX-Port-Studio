# NX Port Studio 1.7.1

1.7.1 brings Nextendo online play to iPhone ports. Animal Crossing: New Horizons is the first game I have played online on my iPhone: the phone opened its island with a Dodo Code and my Mac flew there. The [games table](https://github.com/RobiiAragon/NX-Port-Studio/blob/v1.7.1/README.md#games-with-online-and-local-play) says exactly what I have tested for each game.

## What I played

| Game | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Animal Crossing: New Horizons 3.0.3, iPhone and Mac** | My iPhone opened its island with a Dodo Code and my Mac visited it. The Mac's trace shows Nextendo's matchmaking calls succeeding and about 6,600 datagrams exchanged directly with the phone, with no relay. |

## Nextendo on iPhone

- **Bundled routes.** An iPhone port never went online, signed in or not: its log printed `boot: Nextendo routing status 3`. The host accepted a routes or certificate file only from its own user or root, and iOS installs an app's bundle as another user, so it published no routes. A file inside the app's signed bundle may now have another owner; group- and world-writable files are still refused, and outside the bundle the owner check is unchanged ([reference entry](https://github.com/RobiiAragon/NX-Port-Studio/blob/v1.7.1/docs/reference/services.md#route-files-in-an-ios-bundle-are-owned-by-another-user)). This applies to every iPhone port that uses Nextendo.
- **One account per device.** My first visit ended in Resetti's connection error because both ports were signed in to the same Nextendo account. Ports that play together need one account each. When a port changes account, Animal Crossing offers to delete only its online-feature data (best friends, the Dream Address, Custom Designs Portal posts); the island and the resident stay.

## What I checked

- The route loader's tests with files owned by another user, with and without ASan and UBSan, the documentation tests, and the pull request's CI.
- My Animal Crossing iPhone port rebuilt from this code, signed through Xcode and installed on my iPhone, played as above.

## Limits

- Animal Crossing on iPhone: the test ran on my home Wi-Fi, with the Mac on the same network. I have not had the iPhone visit an island or play with someone over the internet.
- Online play on iPhone is untested for the other games; LAN and LDN are qualified only for Mario Kart 8 Deluxe 3.0.5.
- The phone reached thermal state 2 (serious) after about eight minutes of local play.
- Studio has a local ad-hoc signature and is not notarized. iPhone exports use the creator's own Xcode signing.
- **Rebuild a port to get these changes;** updating Studio does not update an exported game.

I include source, original UI and third-party notices only. Game data, firmware, keys, personal saves and account credentials remain private.
